Muse

An AI manager for independent musicians.

Muse handles the business side of a music career so artists can spend more time on the music. Tell it what you need in plain language and it takes care of:

  • Calendar: booking gigs and sessions, and catching scheduling conflicts.
  • Email: reading booking inquiries and drafting replies for you to review and send.
  • Invoices: creating invoices and tracking who has paid.
  • Social posts: drafting posts that sound like you.
  • Contacts: keeping notes on venues, promoters and collaborators.

Muse is in private testing and is not open for sign-ups yet.
