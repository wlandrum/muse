The problem

Being independent means being your own manager.

Every show comes with work nobody sees. Booking emails, calendar juggling, promo posts, the invoice you meant to send and the payment you still haven't chased.

Most independent artists can't afford a manager, so all of it lands on you, usually at 1 AM after the gig.

  • Check the calendar

How it works

From offer to show night in three steps.

  1. Send Muse the offer

    Forward the email or type it in your own words. Muse pulls out the date, venue, load-in, set time, pay and contact.

  2. Muse checks your calendar

    It adds the gig and tells you about any clash with a session, rehearsal or lesson, with options to fix it.

  3. You approve what goes out

    Confirmations, promo posts and invoices show up as drafts. Nothing is sent or posted until you say so.

What Muse does

One manager for the business side of your music.

Calendar

Every gig, fully filled in

Load-in, soundcheck, set time, pay and contact on each event, with a reminder before the show that has the address and your gear notes.

Clashes

Catch conflicts early

Muse spots overlaps with sessions, rehearsals and lessons before you commit, and suggests ways to make it work.

Inbox

Booking mail, sorted

Finds gig offers and session requests in your email, pulls out the details and drafts replies that sound like you.

Promo

Posts in your voice

Show announcements and thank-you posts written from how you already post, ready for you to edit and share.

Invoices

Get paid on time

Builds the invoice from the gig details, tracks what's paid and what's overdue, and drafts a friendly reminder.

Records

Venues and earnings

Keeps your venues, contacts and gig history in one place, with an earnings summary when tax time comes.

Muse is still being built, so features may change before launch.

Muse drafts. You decide.

FAQ

Questions artists ask.

What is Muse?

Muse is an AI manager for independent artists. It handles the admin around your gigs: scheduling, clashes, booking emails, promo posts and invoices.

Who is it for?

Independent musicians, bands and session players who book and run their own shows.

Will Muse send or post anything without asking me?

No. Every email, post and invoice stays a draft until you approve it.

What will it connect to?

Muse is being built to work with Google Calendar and Gmail, with Instagram and TikTok for promo posts.

How much will it cost?

Pricing isn't set yet.

When can I use it?

Muse is in private testing with a few artists, and there's no public launch date yet.
