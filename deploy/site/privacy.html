Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Muse is an AI manager for independent musicians. This page explains what information Muse uses, why, and what control you have over it.

What Muse accesses

When you connect your Google account, you choose to give Muse access to:

  • Google Calendar: to show your schedule, check for conflicts, and add or change events you ask for.
  • Gmail: to read messages so it can summarize them and spot booking inquiries, and to save reply drafts in your Gmail Drafts folder. Muse does not send email; you review and send drafts yourself.

Muse also stores what you create in it: contacts and notes, invoices, social post drafts, and writing samples used to match your voice.

How it is used

Your information is used only to provide Muse's features to you. To understand your requests, Muse sends the relevant parts of your messages, calendar and emails to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which processes them and returns a response. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

Google user data

Muse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train AI models, and no person reads it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on a private server in Google Cloud in the United States, encrypted in transit with HTTPS, and protected by a password. The connection to your Google account is stored on that server so you do not have to reconnect each visit.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google at any time from Muse's sidebar, or from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask us to delete your data and we will remove it from the server and its backups within 30 days.
